# Ben Oxford Hub artwork

The SVG files in `public/brand` are unchanged copies of the owner's supplied logos.

- `boh-logo.svg`: horizontal logo, from `file logo-01golden.svg`.
- `boh-stacked.svg`: stacked logo, from `file logo-034(1).svg`.

Interface brand colours sampled from the SVG artwork: navy `#0B2E4B`, burgundy `#7F1337`, gold `#CDB767`. White remains the main reading surface. These are app theme selections, not a separate owner-supplied colour specification.

Payment and attendance status colours are semantic and independent of branding. Do not recolour the original logos or replace status meanings with brand colours.
